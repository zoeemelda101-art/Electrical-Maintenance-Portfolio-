⚡ Electrical Maintenance

Electrical maintenance activities focused on the reliability, safety, and availability of industrial electrical equipment and systems.

Preventive & Corrective Maintenance

- Preventive and corrective maintenance of low-voltage electrical installations.
- Inspection and maintenance of electrical panels, circuit breakers, control circuits, lighting systems, sockets, and power cables.
- Troubleshooting of electrical faults and identification of root causes.
- Electrical equipment inspection before and after maintenance activities.
- Maintenance preparation, work planning, and execution according to approved procedures.

Electrical Testing & Measurements

- Insulation resistance testing using a Megger.
- Continuity and electrical circuit verification.
- Voltage, current, and phase sequence measurements.
- Earth resistance testing.
- Loop impedance testing.
- Contact resistance measurements.
- Micro-ohmmeter testing for electrical equipment.
- High-voltage (Hi-Pot) testing where applicable.
- Verification and recording of test results.

Safety & Work Practices

- Application of electrical isolation and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) principles.
- Electrical safety verification before maintenance activities.
- Compliance with approved procedures, work permits, and risk-control measures.
- Use of calibrated electrical test instruments.
- Identification and reporting of electrical defects and safety deviations.

Industrial Electrical Equipment

- Low-voltage distribution systems
- Electrical panels and switchboards
- Circuit breakers and protection devices
- Motors and motor control systems
- Transformers and associated equipment
- Power and control cables
- Lighting and socket systems
- ATS and electrical supply systems

Documentation & Quality

- Completion of maintenance checklists and inspection records.
- Recording of test results and equipment condition.
- Preparation of maintenance reports.
- Follow-up of identified defects and corrective actions.
- Traceability of maintenance activities and equipment history.
